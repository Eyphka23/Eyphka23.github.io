26.6.30

背

题目：
穷游（travel on budget）是目前一种时尚的旅游方式，穷游族会上网查好攻略，对旅游中的吃、住、行精打细算，这种旅游方式越来越受到年轻人的喜爱。你是否选择这种方式旅游？请做出选择，并说明理由。

作文：
Travelling on budget is gaining increasing popularity, especially among young people. Budget travelers will first gather adequate information from the Internet and then work out a pocket-friendly travel plan, optimizing every cent of the budget to maximize the joy of travel. As far as I am concerned, I will certainly follow the trend for the following reasons.

First of all, travelling on budget is not only cost-saving but also allows me absolute freedom. By finding handy guidebooks and reading travellers’ comments online, I can craft carefully every leg of my money-saving trip and I am free to make a flexible and individualized schedule to satisfy my personal preference and demands. Free from those so-called standard routes, I can skip those fancy meals, expensive direct flights and luxurious hotels. Instead, I can try something new and fun, which will not necessarily cost much but cater to my desire for excitement and adventure. Free from a fixed schedule, I won’t have to rush from one place to another and wait in long lines at scenic spots just to have a glimpse. Rather, I can travel at my own pace and go to see whatever attracts me.

What’s more, budget travelling can improve life skills and enhance independence, which are very essential when I step out of campus into the adult world. Since everything about the trip is on my own, I have to book flight tickets, hotels and find places to eat at very reasonable prices. And of course, I will deal with uncertainty and meet different people along the trip.

In conclusion, travelling is no longer a luxury limited to the rich. Budget travelling facilitates a fun and fruitful life experience with its cost-effectiveness and freedom for young people, who are on a tight budget. Let’s join budget travelers and pursue the spirit of exploring the world.

写

题目：
假设你是明启中学高三学生李明，近日有一位外国视频博主 Mike 正在中国旅行，他计划制作一期关于中国的视频。现在他向广大网友征集相关视频素材，视频打算聚焦中国近年来的快速发展与变化，例如：高铁铁路（high-speed trains）、共享单车（shared bicycles）、移动支付（mobile payment）、网上购物（online shopping）、外卖餐饮（food delivery）等。 请你给他写一封邮件，为他提供具体的素材建议。
内容须包括：
(1) 推荐你认为最具代表性的素材（1—2个）；
(2) 结合自身经历给出理由。

作文：
Dear Mike:

I am writing to offer my suggestions on the materials for your up coming video. In my opinion, high-speed trains and mobile payment should be definitely your top choices.

Highly regarded as the “name card” of China, high-speed trains serve as prime symbol of China’s rapid development. Thanks to their incredible efficiency, we can reach almost any destination in a flash. Personally I used to dread travelling due to the long distances. However, with these modern trains, the geological distance seems to disappear entirely.

Meanwhile, mobile payment is another remarkable milestone. Freeing us from carrying cashes and credit cards, it allows us to make transactions simply with a smart phone, which brings us boundaryless convenience.I still vividly remember a previous trip where I had to spend ages calculating prices and waiting for change.Nearly 5 minutes were wasted in that tedious process – a sharp contrast to the mere seconds it takes now.

In conclusion, high-speed trains and mobile payment are perfect epitome of modern China, making them the ideal themes in your video.Thanks you for your time and patience and I am looking forward to watching your fantastic video!

Yours,
Daniel

背

题目：
描述你在高中阶段的一次难忘的活动。具体要求如下：
① 详细描述活动的情况；
② 简要表达你在活动中的感受。

作文：
Thursday, December 23rd, 2021, was the most unforgettable day in Senior Three for me. It was the first time, as well as the last time that I had performed on the stage of our school at the closing ceremony of the School Art Festival.

As scheduled, the ceremony began at 7 o’clock that evening and everyone felt excited, awaiting the wonderful performances. In fact, the performances were undoubtedly sensationally terrific, but I just couldn’t be fully absorbed in them. I was a bit nervous. At the summon of the teacher, nearly 200 senior three students, including me, went to the back of the stage to prepare for our singing performance. Everyone took his place and after that, I couldn’t help thinking that it was my last art festival and that I should spare no efforts to add a brilliant touch to it.

Slowly, the curtain was drawn up. There burst out a loud applause in the audience, louder than whatever I’d heard. All the people were expecting a great deal of us and we were standing still, every muscle ready. At the sound of the music, we threw all the nervousness into the wind and sang to our heart’s content. Together we sang out our passion, our belief and our dreams. The charm of the song fascinated everyone and our resounding voice lasted in the auditorium. Absolutely, we sang far better than any of the previous rehearsals and our performance turned out a success.

Very soon, we’ll farewell our beloved Alma Mater and headed into our bright future. However, the unforgettable chorus performance with other senior three students will impress itself upon my mind permanently.

写

题目：
假设你是明启中学高三学生李明，收到年龄相仿的英国笔友 Mary 的邮件（如下）。她将于下周末在家中接待来自中国的几位同龄人，但不知道该做些什么准备。请回复她的邮件，内容须包括： (1) 你的建议；
(2) 你的理由。

Subject: Seeking Advice on Hosting Friends from China

Dear Li Ming,

I hope you’re well! I’m reaching out because I’ll be hosting a few friends from China at my home next weekend, and I don’t know what preparations I should make to ensure they feel comfortable and welcome. Do you have any suggestions?

Thank you in advance for any advice you can share! Best, Mary

作文：
Dear Mary,

I’m doing well, and it’s great to hear from you! I’m more than happy to offer some practical suggestions on preparing for your Chinese friends’ visit.

First, you can arrange a tea party. Regarded as the “calling card” of China, tea is undoubtedly the best choice to cater to their tastes. Besides, afternoon tea holds an important place in British culture, making it a perfect blend of both heritages that everyone can enjoy while promoting your own culture.

Second, you could wear hanfu, a traditional Chinese costume. This is a fantastic way to show your hospitality and genuine interest in Chinese culture, which will surely bring you closer together and surprise your guests.

In brief, these preparations will definitely make your Chinese friends feel comfortable and valued. Wish you all an unforgettable experience!

Best,
Li Ming
