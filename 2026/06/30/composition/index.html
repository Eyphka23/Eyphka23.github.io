26.6.30

题目：
假设你是明启中学高三学生李明，近日有一位外国视频博主 Mike 正在中国旅行，他计划制作一期关于中国的视频。现在他向广大网友征集相关视频素材，视频打算聚焦中国近年来的快速发展与变化，例如：高铁铁路（high-speed trains）、共享单车（shared bicycles）、移动支付（mobile payment）、网上购物（online shopping）、外卖餐饮（food delivery）等。 请你给他写一封邮件，为他提供具体的素材建议。
内容须包括：
(1) 推荐你认为最具代表性的素材（1—2个）；
(2) 结合自身经历给出理由。

作文：
Dear Mike:

I am writing to offer my suggestions on the materials for your up coming video.In my opinion, high-speed trains and mobile payment should be definitely your top choices.

Highly regarded as the “name card” of China, high-speed trains serve as prime symbol of China’s rapid development.Thanks to their incredible efficiency, we can reach almost any destination in a flash.Personally I used to dread travelling due to the long distances.However, with these modern trains,the geological distance seems to disappear entirely.

Meanwhile, mobile payment is another remarkable milestone.Freeing us from carrying cashes and credit cards, it allows us to make transactions simply with a smart phone, which brings us boundaryless convenience.I still vividly remember a previous trip where I had to spend ages calculating prices and waiting for change.Nearly 5 minutes were wasted in that tedious process – a sharp contrast to the mere seconds it takes now.

In conclusion, high-speed trains and mobile payment are perfect epitome of modern China, making them the ideal themes in your video.Thanks you for your time and patience and I am looking forward to watching your fantastic video!

Yours
Daniel
